---
name: Cat-Cow
summary: Spinal mobilisation in a quadruped position — alternates between rounded and arched back.
instructions:
  - Start on hands and knees — hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  - 'Inhale, cow: drop the belly, lift the chest and gaze up and forward.'
  - 'Exhale, cat: round the spine to the ceiling, chin to chest, tail tucked.'
  - Move with the breath for 8-12 reps — no fast, jerky transitions.
tips:
  - Perfect before and after long sitting — mobilises the whole spine.
  - Movement should flow, not snap between end positions.
---

<h2>The simplest spinal mobilization</h2>
<p>
  Cat-cow moves the spine between the two end positions it should master: full rounding and full
  extension. For people who spend the day sitting, this is the single most effective minute of movement
  there is — sitting holds the spine in one position for hours, and that's exactly what it likes least.
</p>
<p>
  The exercise doubles as a good test: if you can't round your back into an arch, or if extending only
  bends at the lower back, you have a stiff thoracic spine — the most common mobility bottleneck of all.
</p>
<h2>Execution</h2>
<p>
  Quadruped position, hands under the shoulders, knees under the hips. On the exhale, round the back
  upward, chin to chest, pelvis tucking under. On the inhale, reverse it: sternum forward, pelvis tilting
  forward, gaze lifting slightly.
</p>
<p>
  What matters is that the movement travels through the entire spine — vertebra by vertebra, not just at
  the neck and lower back.
</p>
<h2>Common mistakes</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Only moving the ends.</strong> Head up, hips up, middle rigid — the usual pattern, and exactly the area that needs the movement.</li>
  <li><strong>Too fast.</strong> Four to six seconds per direction, paced with the breath.</li>
  <li><strong>Pushing into pain.</strong> The movement should feel good; reaching the absolute end range isn't the goal.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Programming</h2>
<p>
  8 to 12 slow cycles, in a warm-up or as a break during the workday. Fine and useful to do daily.
</p>
